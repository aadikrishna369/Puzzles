Puzzles started with a simple idea: everyday tasks shouldn’t need heavy software, a sign-up, or a tutorial.

Our story

Every annoying little job — merging photos into a document, resizing an image, solving something fiddly — is a puzzle with one missing piece. We build that piece: a focused tool that does exactly one thing, quickly and cleanly.

Each tool lives in its own folder, is easy to maintain, and plugs straight into the Puzzles hub. That’s how the toolbox keeps growing without getting messy.
